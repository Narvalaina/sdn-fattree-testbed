# Exploratory ECMP work

The following scripts are intentionally retained as **exploratory engineering evidence**, not as a claim of a completed full Fat-Tree ECMP implementation:

- `ch08_ecmp_full_datapath_demo.py`
- `ch08_ecmp_full_restconf_attempt.py`
- `ch08_process_ecmp_full_phase_d.py`

The validated main campaign uses deterministic RESTCONF forwarding. The bounded ECMP extension is represented by the limited/select-group experiments. See `docs/limitations.md`.
